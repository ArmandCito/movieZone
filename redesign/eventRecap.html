Archived Recap

Solstice Community Feast & Acoustic Night

calendar_today Saturday, 20 December 2025
location_on Nyarutarama Lakeside, Kigali
group 210+ Gathered

Under string lights by the water, over two hundred of us gathered to close out the year with live acoustic sets, communal dining, and unbeatable Kigali energy. Through every plate shared and every beverage ordered, this single evening raised enough direct proceeds to cover full school fees, books, and uniforms for 42 primary school students across Gasabo district. Thank you to everyone who showed up and made an indelible difference.

42 Children Enrolled for Term 1, 2026

100% of school tuition, classroom exercise books, pens, and custom tailored uniforms disbursed in full partnership with Kigali Community Schools Initiative.
